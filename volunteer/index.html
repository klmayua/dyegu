Community · Founding Volunteer Charter

Give an hour to someone starting over.

You've spent years learning what most people only discover the hard way. Offer one focused hour a month, free, to people rebuilding after job loss, business failure, divorce, illness or grief.

I

Lead a session

45 minutes on one practical subject, then Q&A. We handle scheduling, hosting, moderation and audience.

II

Host a circle

Six weekly calls with 6–8 peers at the same stage. Structured facilitator guide and onboarding provided.

III

Answer in writing

Reply to a reader question for The Comeback File, credited by name and profession, monthly or quarterly.

Approved volunteers are credited by name and profession. Pitching, selling or taking clients from sessions is strictly prohibited under our guidelines.

Apply to volunteer

We review every application manually and verify identity, experience and regulated licences. We reply within two weeks.
