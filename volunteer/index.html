Give an hour to someone starting over.

You've spent years learning what most people only find out the hard way. Give a little of it, for free, to people rebuilding after a job loss, a failed business, a divorce, a health setback or grief.

I

Lead a session

45 minutes on one practical topic, then Q&A. We handle the scheduling, the room and the audience.

II

Host a circle

Six weekly calls with six to eight people at the same stage. Short training and a host guide provided.

III

Answer in writing

Reply to a reader question for the Comeback File, credited to you, once a month or once a quarter.

Volunteers are credited by name and profession on the site and in the Comeback File. Selling, pitching or taking clients from sessions isn't allowed; full rules are in the guidelines.

Apply to volunteer

We review every application by hand and check identity, experience and, for regulated professions, your licence. We'll reply within two weeks.
