About Sir K & DYEGU

Why DYEGU exists.

Sir K has spent more than twenty years building companies, teams and technology across Africa and beyond — and has had to rebuild more than once. When things went wrong, what helped most wasn't slogans. It was seeing proof that someone else had stood in the rubble and found a real way back.

DYEGU — short for Don't You Ever Give Up — is that proof, assembled with journalistic discipline: real people, real setbacks, primary-checked facts, and the honest craft of rebuilding.

Editorial & studio method

How every episode is built

  1. Stage 01 · Research

    Primary records first

    Every claim goes into an episode claims file with its source: filings, court and archival records [V], established reporting [E], or clearly attributed testimony [T].

  2. Stage 02 · Gate 1A / 1B Audit

    Verify or cut

    Anything we cannot confirm is held back and excluded from the script. Sensitive themes stay factual and dignified — the comeback leads the title, never the wound.

  3. Stage 03 · Studio Production

    Human voice, zero synthetic shortcuts

    Sir K presents on camera and narrates every episode in his own voice. Research is done with AI assistance and reviewed by people against the sources published on each story's page.
