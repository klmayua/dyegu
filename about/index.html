About

Sir K, and why DYEGU exists

Sir K has spent more than twenty years building companies, teams and technology across Africa and beyond, and has had to rebuild more than once. When things went wrong, what helped most wasn't advice. It was knowing that someone else had been somewhere worse and found a way back.

DYEGU, short for Don't You Ever Give Up, is that reminder, made carefully: real people, real setbacks, documented facts, and the honest work of coming back.

How each story is made

  1. Research

    Every claim goes into a claims file with its source: primary records first, then reputable reporting, then the person's own words, clearly attributed.

  2. Check

    Anything that can't be confirmed is held back. Sensitive subjects follow strict rules: no invented details, no graphic reconstruction, the comeback in the title, not the wound.

  3. Tell

    Sir K presents and narrates every episode himself. Research is done with AI assistance and checked by people against the sources, which are published on each story's page.
