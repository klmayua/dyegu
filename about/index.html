About DYEGU

Every person rebuilding is the hero of this story.

DYEGU — Don't You Ever Give Up — is not a self-help cult, a stage for a miracle worker, or a motivational megaphone. It is a quiet, primary-sourced record of how ordinary and well-known people survived public collapse — and a free community so nobody has to rebuild alone.

Who started this

Not a guru. Just another person rebuilding.

I'm not a motivational speaker, a preacher, or a miracle worker. I've spent twenty years building teams and companies — and I've had to pick up the pieces and rebuild more than once, and I'm still rebuilding today.

When things fell apart for me, loud slogans only made me feel more alone. What actually steadied me was reading honest, unvarnished proof of how long other people's wilderness years really lasted — and seeing that ordinary endurance works. DYEGU is simply that room, built carefully for anyone walking through a hard season right now.

“Until the next story… don't you ever give up.”

How every story is verified

Three rules before a word is published

  1. Step 01 · Primary records first

    Check the dates and filings

    Every claim enters a claims file tagged as primary record [V], established reporting [E], or the subject's own words [T].

  2. Step 02 · Hold back the uncertain

    Never invent drama

    Anything we cannot confirm is cut. Sensitive moments are treated with restraint and dignity — the rebuild leads, never the wound.

  3. Step 03 · Publish every receipt

    Open to your scrutiny

    Every story dossier publishes its complete source list openly so you never have to take anyone's word on faith.
