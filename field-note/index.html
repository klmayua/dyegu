DYEGU · The Wilderness Field Note

Put the facts on one clean page.

Before a single comeback in Season 1 began, someone separated what actually happened from the verdict looping in their head at 2 a.m. Take ten quiet minutes with a pen.

1. The Two-Column Reality Audit

Column A · Documented Facts

Write 5 lines a journalist could verify with dates and figures. Zero adjectives about your worth.

Column B · The Verdict You Added

Write the private sentences looping at 2 a.m. (“I ruined everything”, “I’m too late”). Draw one straight line through each.

2. What Survived the Wreck

Three things nobody can repossess

Skills, judgement, hard-won lessons, or craft you still carry.

Three people who know your work

Not to beg — one fifteen-minute honest conversation this week.

One finishable move for the next 7 days

Not a five-year plan. One phone call, one draft, one boundary, or one clean sheet of numbers you can finish before Sunday.

3. Perspective Check · How Long the Wilderness Lasted

    Stabbed on court in Hamburg → 1996 Australian Open champion
    28 months
  • J.K. Rowling

  • Shania Twain

  • George Foreman

    20 years
