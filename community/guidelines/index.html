Safety & guidelines

DYEGU's circles and sessions exist so people rebuilding after a setback can get support and practical guidance, free. These rules keep them safe and useful. Everyone who joins or volunteers agrees to them.

What this is, and isn't

  • Circles are peer support. Sessions are general guidance for a group.
  • Neither is therapy, medical care, or legal, financial or tax advice for your individual situation. For that, see a qualified professional you engage directly.
  • Neither is an emergency service. If you're in danger or thinking about suicide or self-harm, contact a local crisis line or emergency services now: findahelpline.com.

For everyone

  • Confidentiality. What people share in a circle stays in the circle. No recording, screenshots or quoting without everyone's consent.
  • Respect. No harassment, discrimination, hate or sexual content. Disagree with ideas, never attack people.
  • No selling. No promoting products, services, schemes, loans or investments, and no asking members for money.
  • Your privacy. Share only what you're comfortable with. Don't share other people's private details.
  • Hosts can remove anyone who breaks these rules. Serious breaches are permanent.

Volunteer code

  • General guidance only. Explain options and how things usually work; don't advise on an individual's case in a group, and say when someone needs their own professional.
  • No soliciting. Don't pitch your services, collect contacts or move members into paid work. You may share your name and profession; if someone asks to hire you later, tell the DYEGU team first.
  • Stay in your lane. Speak to what you're qualified in. Regulated professionals follow their own professional rules at all times.
  • Escalate risk. If anyone says they may harm themselves or others, share the crisis-line information, stay calm, and tell the DYEGU team the same day.
  • Protect data. Never keep, copy or reuse members' details outside DYEGU's tools.
  • Adults only. Circles and sessions are for people aged 18 and over.

Reporting a problem

Use the contact form and choose "Something else", or reply to any DYEGU email. We review every report.
