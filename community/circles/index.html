Rebuild Circles

A small group of people at the exact same stage of a setback, meeting online once a week for six weeks. Free, confidential, and hosted by a trained volunteer.

Six-week roadmap

What happens inside a circle

  1. Week 01

    Name the ground

    Meet your 6–8 peers, establish confidentiality, and state honestly where you stand.

  2. Week 02

    Strip the noise

    Separate facts from verdicts using the DYEGU reality audit and one documented story.

  3. Week 03

    Count what remains

    Map transferable skills, allies, and one quiet win you can complete in 7 days.

  4. Week 04

    The long middle

    Tackle the hardest obstacle in your rebuild with honest group feedback.

  5. Week 05

    Build proof

    Make one outward move — an email, a prototype, an application, a boundary.

  6. Week 06

    Your DYEGU rule

    Write the principle you are carrying forward and set your 90-day next milestone.

Not for emergencies. Circles and sessions are peer support and general guidance, not therapy, medical care, or legal or financial advice for your situation.
