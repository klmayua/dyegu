Six-week roadmap

How the six weeks unfold

  1. Week 01

    Name the ground

    Meet 6–8 peers at your chapter, agree to confidentiality, and state honestly where you stand.

  2. Week 02

    Strip the noise

    Run the Two-Column Reality Audit alongside one documented comeback story.

  3. Week 03

    Count what remains

    Map surviving skills, allies, and one quiet win you can finish in 7 days.

  4. Week 04

    The long middle

    Bring the hardest sticking point in your week for honest, lecture-free peer perspective.

  5. Week 05

    Build outward proof

    Complete one concrete outward move — a call, a draft, an application, a boundary.

  6. Week 06

    Your DYEGU rule

    Write the sentence you carry forward and set your next 90-day target.
