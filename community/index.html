How it opens

Calm, vetted, and built for safety

    Join the list for your chapter

    Circles are matched by life stage and time zone band. Sessions are scheduled around the topics requested most.

  2. Step 02

    Every volunteer is vetted by hand

    Identity, experience and regulated licences are verified before anyone hosts. Everyone signs our community guidelines.
