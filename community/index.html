How it opens

Structured, vetted, and built for safety

  1. Step 01

    Join the list for your stage

    Circles are matched by life stage and time zone band. Sessions are scheduled around the topics most people request.

  2. Step 02

    Every volunteer is vetted by hand

    Identity, experience and, for regulated professions, licences are verified before anyone hosts. Everyone signs the community guidelines.

  3. Step 03

    Cohort 01 launches early 2027

    Each circle opens as soon as 6–8 peers in the same stage and time zone band are ready. You'll receive your invitation by email.

Not for emergencies. Circles and sessions are peer support and general guidance, not therapy, medical care, or legal or financial advice for your situation.
