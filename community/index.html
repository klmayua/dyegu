Nobody rebuilds alone.

The stories show it can be done. The community is where you do it: with people at the same stage, and with experts who give their time for free.

How it opens

  1. Join the list for what you need

    Circles are matched by stage and time zone. Sessions are scheduled around the topics most people ask for.

  2. We vet every volunteer

    Identity, experience and, for regulated professions, licences are checked before anyone hosts. Everyone agrees to the community guidelines.

  3. First circles and sessions start in early 2027

    The first circle for a stage opens when enough people in the same time zone have joined. You'll be invited by email.

Not for emergencies. Circles and sessions are peer support and general guidance, not therapy, medical care, or legal or financial advice for your situation.
