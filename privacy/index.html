Privacy

This page explains what dyegu.co collects, why, and what you can ask us to do with it. DYEGU is a documentary series presented by Sir K.

What we collect

  • Your email address, when you sign up for the Comeback File newsletter or the Rebuild Playbook waitlist, with the list you joined and the date.
  • Your name, email, organisation and message, when you send a talk, workshop, sponsorship or press enquiry.

We don't use advertising cookies or tracking pixels on this site, and we don't sell or rent your details to anyone.

Why we use it

  • To send you the newsletter or the launch email you asked for. You can unsubscribe from any email in one click.
  • To reply to your enquiry.

Our legal basis is your consent for emails, and our legitimate interest in answering messages you send us.

Who processes it

Form submissions are stored with Supabase (database, hosted in the UK/EU). Newsletter subscribers are sent through Substack. The site is hosted by Vercel. Episodes embedded from YouTube load only when you press play, using YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode.

How long we keep it

Newsletter and waitlist emails are kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them. Enquiries are kept for up to two years, then deleted.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw consent, at any time. Reply to any email we send you, or use the enquiry form on the home page and choose "Something else". We'll act within 30 days.

If you're in the UK or EU and unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO).
