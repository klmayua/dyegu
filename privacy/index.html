Privacy

This page explains what DYEGU's website collects, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

What we collect

  • Your email address when you join The Comeback File, The Rebuild Playbook waitlist or the shop drop list, with the list you joined, the chapter you chose (if any), the products you ticked, the page you signed up on and the campaign link you arrived from.
  • Your name, email, organisation and message when you send a talk, workshop, sponsorship or press enquiry.
  • Your circle or session request: the stage you choose, your time zone, the topics you tick and any note, so we can match you to a circle or schedule sessions.
  • Volunteer applications: name, email, profession, qualifications, profile link, country, languages and availability, so we can vet and schedule volunteers. Approved volunteers are credited by name and profession.
  • Your story when you share your rebuild, with your first name if you give it and the permissions you tick. Please leave out details that identify other people.
  • Private on-device checklist: ticking off moves on a stage page stores progress strictly in your own browser's localStorage and is never sent to our servers.
  • Anonymous visit statistics (pages viewed, country, device type) through Vercel Web Analytics, which uses no cookies.

We don't use advertising cookies or tracking pixels, and we don't sell or rent your details to anyone.

Why we use it

  • To send the emails you asked for. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe.
  • To reply to enquiries and confirm submissions.
  • To read the stories you share and, only with your permission, feature them. We ask again before publishing anything.
  • To understand which pages help people, so we can improve them.

Our legal basis is your consent for emails and stories, and our legitimate interest in answering messages and running the site.

Who processes it

Form submissions are validated via our Vercel server function and stored with Supabase (database hosted in the UK). Confirmation emails are sent via Resend; weekly newsletter issues are sent through Substack. The site is hosted by Vercel. Episodes embedded from YouTube load only when you press play, using YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode.

How long we keep it

List emails, circle and session requests are kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them. Declined volunteer applications are deleted after six months. Enquiries are kept for up to two years. Shared stories are kept until you ask us to delete them, or for two years if not featured.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw consent, at any time: reply to any email we send you, or use the enquiry form and choose "Something else". We'll act within 30 days. If you're in the UK or EU and unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO).
