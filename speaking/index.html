Short introduction (60 words)

Sir K is the creator and host of DYEGU (Don't You Ever Give Up), a fact-checked documentary series profiling documented comebacks after public loss. With more than twenty years building companies, teams and technology across Africa and beyond, he speaks to organisations, founders and global audiences on resilience grounded in evidence, not slogans.

Conference bio

Sir K has spent more than two decades building companies, teams and technology ventures across Africa and internationally—navigating major growth cycles as well as hard rebuilds firsthand. In 2026 he launched DYEGU (Don't You Ever Give Up), an independent documentary house and free peer-support community dedicated to telling true, primary-sourced stories of people who lost status, health, livelihood or direction and came back. On stage, Sir K brings the same calm baritone, journalistic rigour and practical clarity that define the series—leaving audiences with concrete frameworks for rebuilding rather than temporary motivational hype.
