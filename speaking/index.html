Short introduction (60 words)

Sir K is the creator of DYEGU (Don't You Ever Give Up), an independent documentary house and free rebuilding community dedicated to primary-sourced comebacks after public loss. With more than twenty years building companies, teams and technology across Africa and beyond, he speaks plainly on resilience grounded in evidence, not slogans.

Conference bio

Sir K has spent more than twenty years building companies, teams and technology across Africa and internationally — navigating growth as well as hard rebuilds firsthand. In 2026 he founded DYEGU (Don't You Ever Give Up), a primary-sourced documentary series and free peer-support community built for adults navigating major setbacks. In talks and executive workshops, he skips motivational theatre in favour of documented history and practical clarity.
