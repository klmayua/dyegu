Where are you right now? · My business or money is gone

Your business failed, or the money ran out.

Debt, a closed company, savings gone. Money trouble is loud and private at the same time. People have rebuilt from far less than you have right now.

Three moves for this week

  1. Put the whole number on one page.

    What you owe, to whom, and when it's due. Fear lives in the parts you haven't looked at.

  2. Separate the loss from who you are.

    Write down what the attempt taught you that nobody can repossess: skills, contacts, judgement.

  3. Make one call before they call you.

    Pick one creditor or one partner and ask what a realistic plan looks like. Get qualified advice before signing anything; this page isn't financial advice.
