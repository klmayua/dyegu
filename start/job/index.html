Where are you right now? · I lost my job

You lost your job.

Fired, laid off, pushed out or quietly passed over. It feels personal because work is where a lot of us keep our sense of worth. It is not the end of your story.

Three moves for this week

  1. Write down what happened, facts only.

    One page. Dates, decisions, what you did and didn't control. No verdicts on yourself. It turns a fog into something you can work with.

  2. Message three people who've seen your work.

    Not to ask for a job. Ask for fifteen minutes and one honest view of where you're strongest. Most comebacks start with a conversation, not an application.

  3. Pick one skill for the next 90 days.

    The one that would make you more valuable in the room you want to be in. Book the first hour of it this week.
