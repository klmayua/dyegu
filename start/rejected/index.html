Where are you right now? · Rejected again

You've been rejected. Again.

The application, the audition, the pitch, the publisher. Every no lands on the same bruise. The people in these stories collected a lot of them before the yes.

Three moves for this week

  1. Count the nos as data, not a verdict.

    Keep a list: what you sent, to whom, what came back. Patterns show up that feelings hide.

  2. Ask one person who said no for one reason.

    Short and polite. Most won't answer; the one who does may change your next attempt.

  3. Set a number, not a deadline.

    "Ten more attempts" is in your control. "By March" isn't.
