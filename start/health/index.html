Where are you right now? · A health setback

Your health set you back.

An injury, a diagnosis, a long recovery. When your body changes the plan, everything else can feel on hold. Many of the comebacks in this series started in a hospital.

Three moves for this week

  1. Follow your care team's plan, and ask your questions.

    Write them down before appointments. You're allowed to understand what's happening to you.

  2. Measure progress in small units.

    Days, not months. A walk to the corner counts. Write each one down; on bad days, read the list.

  3. Redraw the timeline, not the goal.

    Most comebacks took longer than anyone expected. Slower is not the same as over.

If it's more than a setback right now: please talk to a doctor, a counsellor or someone you trust. If you're thinking about suicide or self-harm, contact a local crisis line or emergency services now. Find a free helpline in your country.
