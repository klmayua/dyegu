Where are you right now? · Starting over at 40, 50 or later

You're starting over later in life.

40, 50, 60 or beyond, with less time and more responsibility than the first time. Experience is an asset most beginners would pay for.

Three moves for this week

  1. List what you already know how to do.

    Everything, including the things you stopped noticing. You're not starting from zero; you're starting from a different place.

  2. Find one person ten years ahead of you on the new road.

    Ask how they started, and what they'd skip.

  3. Choose a first step you can finish in a month.

    Momentum matters more than the size of the first win.
