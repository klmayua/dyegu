Where are you right now? · Grieving a loss

You're grieving.

A person, a marriage, a career, a version of yourself. Grief doesn't follow a schedule, and nothing here is meant to rush it.

Three moves for this week

  1. Let it be as big as it is.

    You don't need to be fine. Tell someone you trust how it actually is.

  2. Keep one small daily anchor.

    A walk, a meal at the same time, a call. Something steady while the rest isn't.

  3. Get support if it's getting heavier, not lighter.

    A doctor, a counsellor or a grief group can help carry it. Asking is strength, not weakness.

If it's more than a setback right now: please talk to a doctor, a counsellor or someone you trust. If you're thinking about suicide or self-harm, contact a local crisis line or emergency services now. Find a free helpline in your country.
