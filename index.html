The DYEGU standard

Comebacks you can check.

I

Real people

Names you know. People who held real status, health, money or fame, lost it in public, and climbed back without shortcuts.

II

Documented receipts

Every claim is audited against primary records or two independent reports, and every source is published on the story's dossier page.

III

Zero invention

No fabricated inner monologues, no guessed net worths, no sensationalised trauma. The documented truth is dramatic enough.
