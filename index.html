The DYEGU standard

Comebacks you can check.

I

Real people

Names you know. People who held real status, health, money or fame, lost it in public, and climbed back.

II

Documented facts

Every claim is checked before it's narrated, and every source is published on the story's page so you can read it yourself.

III

No invention

No made-up inner thoughts, no guessed net worths, no dramatised quotes. The truth is dramatic enough.

01

Steve Jobs

Forced out of Apple, the company he co-founded, at 30. Eleven years, two companies and a near-bankrupt Apple later, he walked back in.

 02

J.K. Rowling

A single mother on benefits in Edinburgh, grieving, with three chapters and a stack of rejections. Then one reader said yes.

 03

Tyler Perry

He spent his savings on one play. About thirty people came. He slept in his car. He kept staging it, year after year, until the seventh. Today he owns one of America's largest studios.
