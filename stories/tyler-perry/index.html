Tyler Perry

Tyler Perry saved $12,000 and spent all of it staging his first play in Atlanta in 1992. About thirty people came. He lived in his car for three months and kept staging the same play once a year while it failed, until 1998. In 2015 he bought 330 acres of a former army base and built one of America's largest film studios.
