Tyler Perry

Tyler Perry saved $12,000 and spent all of it staging his first play in Atlanta in 1992. About thirty people came. He lived in his car for three months and kept staging the same play once a year while it failed, until 1998. In 2015 he bought 330 acres of a former army base and built one of America's largest film studios.

Three moments from the documentary

Key moment · Mon

“He bet his entire $12,000 savings on one play.”

He expected 1,200 people. About 30 came. He lost his apartment.

Key moment · Wed

“Six foot five. Living in a Geo Metro.”

Three months in that car, hiding it from the repo man, while the same play failed every year.

Key moment · Fri

“He bought 330 acres of an army base.”

$30 million in 2015, about $250 million invested, 12 soundstages. Warner Bros.' lot is 110 acres.
