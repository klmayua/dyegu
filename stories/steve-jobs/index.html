Steve Jobs

In 1985, at 30, Steve Jobs was stripped of every operating role at the company he co-founded and walked out. He spent eleven years building NeXT and funding Pixar through years of losses. Then Toy Story, Pixar's IPO and Apple's purchase of NeXT brought him back to a company that had just lost more than a billion dollars.

Archival turning points

Three moments from the documentary

Key moment · Mon

“His own CEO told Wall Street he'd never work there again.”

Sept 1985: Sculley tells analysts there is no role for Steve Jobs at Apple, today or in the future. Jobs is 30.

Key moment · Wed

“He lost money for ten straight years, then became a billionaire in a day.”

NeXT lost $40–66M a year; Pixar burned through about $50M of his own money. Then Toy Story, and a first day of trading from $22 to $39.

Key moment · Fri

“Michael Dell said: shut Apple down.”

Oct 1997, Apple has just lost $1.05 billion. One year later: the iMac, and a $309 million profit.
