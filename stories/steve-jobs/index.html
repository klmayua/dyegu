Steve Jobs

In 1985, at 30, Steve Jobs was stripped of every operating role at the company he co-founded and walked out. He spent eleven years building NeXT and funding Pixar through years of losses. Then Toy Story, Pixar's IPO and Apple's purchase of NeXT brought him back to a company that had just lost more than a billion dollars.
