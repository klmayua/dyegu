Look inside · Chapter I · Worksheet Excerpt: The Two-Column Reality Audit

Before Steve Jobs built NeXT or J.K. Rowling finished chapter four, they had to separate documented fact from internal catastrophe. Complete this on one quiet page:

Column A — The Documented Facts

Write 5 bullet points a journalist could verify with dates and numbers. No adjectives about your worth.

Column B — The Story You Added

Write the private verdicts running in your head ('I ruined everything', 'I'm too late'). Draw a line through each one.

The Surviving Inventory

List 3 capabilities, 3 people who respect your work, and 1 thing you can finish in the next 7 days.
