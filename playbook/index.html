Look inside · Chapter I · Worksheet Excerpt: The Two-Column Reality Audit

Before Steve Jobs built NeXT or J.K. Rowling finished chapter four, they had to separate what actually happened from the verdict in their head. Complete this on one clean page:

Column A — The Documented Facts

Write 5 lines a journalist could verify with dates and figures. Zero adjectives about your worth.

Column B — The Verdict You Added

Write the private sentences looping at 2 a.m. ('I ruined everything', 'I'm too late'). Draw one straight line through each.

What Survived the Wreck

List 3 capabilities, 3 people who respect your work, and 1 finishable move for the next 7 days.
